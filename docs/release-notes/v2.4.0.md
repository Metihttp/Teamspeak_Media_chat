**TS Media chat 2.4.0** brings link previews to your TeamSpeak chat: YouTube cards, and pictures, GIFs and videos right under their links.

### Highlights

- **YouTube cards.** A YouTube link shows its title, channel and thumbnail; a click opens the video in your browser.
- **Pictures, GIFs and videos from links.** They show and play right in the chat, like files sent with TS Media.
- **Made by the sender.** The sender's plugin stores the preview on your TeamSpeak server, so your PC doesn't contact the website.
- **Other links load by themselves**, within your automatic download limits; or switch to click-to-load.
- **Important fixes install by themselves.** With automatic checks on, a fix signed by the author installs quietly and starts with your next TeamSpeak start.
- **Newer version nearby.** When someone in your channel has a newer TS Media, only you see a hint.

### What's new

- **Link previews**, as asked for in [issue #1](https://github.com/Metihttp/Teamspeak_Media_chat/issues/1): YouTube links (also `youtu.be` and Shorts) get a card, and links to pictures, GIFs and videos show the file right in the chat.
- **Stored on your TeamSpeak server.** When you send such a link in a channel or private chat, your plugin loads it once and shares the preview through the channel's files. Everyone with TS Media sees it without contacting the website.
- **Other links load by themselves.** Links from people without the plugin load their preview from the website when they show on your screen (YouTube always, pictures, GIFs and videos within your automatic download limits); your PC connects to that website. Turn off **Load link previews automatically** to load them only on a click.
- **Your own links show at once**, from your own download, while your plugin shares them.
- **On by default.** Switches in **Settings → Privacy & updates** turn it off, and right-click → **Hide preview** hides one. Behind a proxy-based VPN, previews use your Windows proxy.
- **Important updates install by themselves** with automatic checks on: signed by the author, they start with your next TeamSpeak start, and TeamSpeak is never restarted for them. [How it works](https://github.com/Metihttp/Teamspeak_Media_chat/blob/v2.4.0/docs/UPDATES.md#important-updates).
- **"Someone in this channel has a newer version."** Only you see this hint, and nothing is sent until you click it.

### Fixed

- The update window opened during a download showed an empty page: now it shows the progress.

### Good to know

- Where YouTube is blocked, YouTube previews need a VPN on the sender's PC (or on yours for links without a sender's preview). Previews made by the sender come from your TeamSpeak server and need no VPN.
- Links in the server chat are always loaded by your PC: the sender's preview needs a channel or private chat.
- Web pages (for example `klipy.com/gifs/...` or `tenor.com/view/...`) get no preview: copy the GIF's own address (ending in `.gif`, `.webp` or `.mp4`) instead.
- Who contacts which website, and when: [privacy & security](https://github.com/Metihttp/Teamspeak_Media_chat/blob/v2.4.0/docs/PRIVACY-SECURITY.md#link-previews).

### Install or update

**On 2.2.x or 2.3.x:** it updates itself. Wait for the update offer, or use **Check for updates…** in the plugin menu. Your settings and cache are kept.

**On 2.1 or older, or new here?** Close TeamSpeak, double-click the file above, then type `/tsmedia` in the chat.

### Known issues

- The 32-bit DLL is untested on a real 32-bit client.
- TeamSpeak may crash or hang when it quits, with or without the plugin: [workaround](https://github.com/Metihttp/Teamspeak_Media_chat/blob/main/docs/FAQ.md#teamspeak-crashes-or-hangs-when-i-quit).
