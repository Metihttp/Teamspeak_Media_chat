**TS Media chat 2.7.2** fixes the small dark mode leftovers of 2.7.1, opens background chats in their final look, and shows *Load preview* on links that wait for their turn.

### Highlights

- **Nothing dark left behind.** Disabling or reloading TS Media with dark mode on draws every picture and card it drew dark in your style's colors, even huge ones.
- **Your text style back too.** When dark mode goes off, names that came in meanwhile get your style's bold, italic, underline and size back, not just its colors.
- **Calmer link previews.** A link that waits for its turn to load by itself shows *Load preview* instead of a loading ring; a click loads it at once.

### Fixed

- Dark mode: turning TS Media off in **Options → Addons** (Disable or Reload) while dark mode is on now draws every picture and card it drew in dark mode in your style's colors, also very large pictures and long chats full of pictures. Before, a picture TS Media couldn't read again quickly at that moment kept its dark look in the light chat.
- Dark mode: names, channels, history lines, server errors, host names and times that came in while dark mode was on get your style's text style back when it goes off (or TS Media is turned off): bold, italic, underlined and in your style's size. Before, only their colors came back, so with a style whose names aren't bold a name stayed bold.
- Dark mode: an info panel TeamSpeak showed while dark mode was on gets your style's look back when dark mode goes off: its labels (*Version:*, *Online since:*, …) no longer come back in the host name color where your style gives them no color, and its title and the custom nickname get your style's size. With another style than TeamSpeak's default style and the community dark skin, some of the panel's spacing can stay dark mode's until you select something else.
- Dark mode: a chat that opened while dark mode was on (a new server tab or private chat, and every chat when TeamSpeak started with dark mode on) gets your style's look when dark mode goes off, also in the lines that come in later. Before, it kept TeamSpeak's own dark chat colors until TeamSpeak restarted. After you picked a style in **Tools → Options → Design** while dark mode was on, the chat tab names no longer turn white on the light tab bar when it goes off.
- Dark mode: a chat whose tab was in the background while you turned dark mode on or off opens in its final layout at once, without a sideways scroll bar or messages that move for a moment. The chat column keeps the width your style gives it in dark mode too.
- Dark mode: turning it on or off with the Settings window open is faster, and the window changes its look once instead of two or three times.
- Dark mode: when another program or plugin replaces TeamSpeak's whole color palette while dark mode is on, the *TS Media chat* lines printed meanwhile still get your style's colors back when dark mode goes off.
- Chat zoom: after you change the chat zoom while a chat is in the background, that chat opens with the message you were reading in the same place from the first moment; a chat at its end opens at its end.
- Link previews: a link that waits for its turn to load by itself (links load 2 at a time, one per person) shows **Load preview** while it waits (a click loads it at once), and the loading ring only once its preview really loads. Before, such links showed the loading ring, also for a long time when many links came in at once.

### Install or update

**On 2.2.1 or later (2.2.1, 2.3.x, 2.4.0, 2.5.0, 2.6.0, 2.7.0 or 2.7.1):** it updates itself. Wait for the update offer, or use **Check for updates…** in the plugin menu. Your settings, cache and GIFs are kept. On 2.2.0 it updates to 2.2.1 first, then to 2.7.2.

**On 2.1 or older, or new here?** Close TeamSpeak, double-click the file above, then type `/tsmedia` in the chat.

### Known issues

- The 32-bit DLL is untested on a real 32-bit client, and it has no YouTube player (Play opens your browser).
- In dark mode, web pages inside TeamSpeak (the Addons browser, myTeamSpeak), the YouTube player's page and a few names TeamSpeak colors in the channel tree (friends, blocked people, someone recording) keep their own colors: [why](https://github.com/Metihttp/Teamspeak_Media_chat/blob/v2.7.2/docs/FAQ.md#some-names-in-the-channel-tree-keep-their-colors-in-dark-mode).
- With *Use Windows proxy settings*, a Windows setup script that answers with a SOCKS proxy can still send the update check directly, and the YouTube player through SOCKS with names looked up on your PC; the chat proxy avoids this.
- TeamSpeak may crash or hang when it quits, with or without the plugin: [workaround](https://github.com/Metihttp/Teamspeak_Media_chat/blob/main/docs/FAQ.md#teamspeak-crashes-or-hangs-when-i-quit).
