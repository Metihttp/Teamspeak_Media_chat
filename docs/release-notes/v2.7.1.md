**TS Media chat 2.7.1** fixes the two small dark mode leftovers of 2.7.0 and opens a new server's chat at its newest messages.

### Highlights

- **Your colors back after dark mode.** When dark mode goes off, TS Media's own lines come back in exactly your style's colors, the bold *TS Media chat* too.
- **No dark cards left behind.** Turning TS Media off or reloading it with dark mode on draws its cards and pictures again in your style's colors.
- **New chats open at the end.** A server's chat when you connect, a new tab or a private chat opens at its newest messages, not at its first line.

### Fixed

- Dark mode: the *TS Media chat* lines printed while dark mode was on get exactly your TeamSpeak style's colors when it goes off (the bold *TS Media chat*, the text and the links), with every choice of *Color of messages from TS Media*. Before, the bold *TS Media chat* of a line printed with **Same as the chat text** came back in the name color.
- Dark mode: turning TS Media off in **Options → Addons** (Disable or Reload) while dark mode is on no longer leaves pictures and cards drawn in dark colors in the light chat, also in chats that weren't shown (a picture TS Media can't read again quickly at that moment keeps the picture it had). Turning dark mode on or off redraws the pictures and cards, the reply lines, the spoiler bars and the music bar at once, in Cozy, Compact and TeamSpeak classic, and an album's file names no longer keep dark mode's link color.
- Dark mode: a link or colored text inside a text spoiler you revealed now changes color with dark mode like the rest of the message.
- A chat that filled while it wasn't shown now opens at its newest messages: the channel chat of a server you connect to (at TeamSpeak's start, with a bookmark or in a new tab), a private chat that an incoming message opened, a hidden chat after **Options → Addons → Reload**. Before, it could open at its very first line, or a little above its end.
- Chat zoom: a chat you zoomed with Ctrl + mouse wheel and then left for another chat tab no longer grows by one step after **Options → Addons → Reload** or turning TS Media off and on.

### Install or update

**On 2.2.1 or later (2.2.1, 2.3.x, 2.4.0, 2.5.0, 2.6.0 or 2.7.0):** it updates itself. Wait for the update offer, or use **Check for updates…** in the plugin menu. Your settings, cache and GIFs are kept. On 2.2.0 it updates to 2.2.1 first, then to 2.7.1.

**On 2.1 or older, or new here?** Close TeamSpeak, double-click the file above, then type `/tsmedia` in the chat.

### Known issues

- The 32-bit DLL is untested on a real 32-bit client, and it has no YouTube player (Play opens your browser).
- In dark mode, web pages inside TeamSpeak (the Addons browser, myTeamSpeak), the YouTube player's page and a few names TeamSpeak colors in the channel tree (friends, blocked people, someone recording) keep their own colors: [why](https://github.com/Metihttp/Teamspeak_Media_chat/blob/v2.7.1/docs/FAQ.md#some-names-in-the-channel-tree-keep-their-colors-in-dark-mode).
- With *Use Windows proxy settings*, a Windows setup script that answers with a SOCKS proxy can still send the update check directly, and the YouTube player through SOCKS with names looked up on your PC; the chat proxy avoids this.
- TeamSpeak may crash or hang when it quits, with or without the plugin: [workaround](https://github.com/Metihttp/Teamspeak_Media_chat/blob/main/docs/FAQ.md#teamspeak-crashes-or-hangs-when-i-quit).
