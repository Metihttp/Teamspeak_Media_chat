**TS Media chat 2.3.1**: your messages now sit on the right, like in Telegram, and the chat keeps a little room above its tabs.

### Highlights

- **Your messages on the right.** Like Telegram: yours on the right, everyone else's on the left, without your name or avatar.
- **The time in the bubble.** Each of your bubbles is as wide as its message and shows the time right on it.
- **Right-to-left text too.** Persian, Arabic or Hebrew messages of yours are right-aligned, and so are their reactions.
- **Room above the chat tabs.** The newest message no longer touches the tabs under the chat.
- **Your choice.** Prefer the old look? Turn off Show my messages on the right in the Chat layout settings.

### Fixed

- The newest message touched the chat tabs under the chat: now the chat keeps a little room under it and stays at its end when new messages arrive.

### Good to know

- Your messages on the right come with the Cozy layout with bubbles, the default (**Settings → General → Chat layout**). Compact and TeamSpeak classic look as before.
- Only your own view changes: nobody else sees a difference.

### Install or update

**On 2.2.x or 2.3.0:** it updates itself. Wait for the update offer, or use **Check for updates…** in the plugin menu. Your settings and cache are kept.

**On 2.1 or older, or new here?** Close TeamSpeak, double-click the file above, then type `/tsmedia` in the chat.

### Known issues

- The 32-bit DLL is untested on a real 32-bit client.
- TeamSpeak may crash or hang when it quits, with or without the plugin: [workaround](https://github.com/Metihttp/Teamspeak_Media_chat/blob/main/docs/FAQ.md#teamspeak-crashes-or-hangs-when-i-quit).
