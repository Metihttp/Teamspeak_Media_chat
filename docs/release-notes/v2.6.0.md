**TS Media chat 2.6.0** adds a chat proxy: where YouTube and other sites are filtered, links in the chat load through your VPN app's proxy, while TeamSpeak keeps its direct connection to your server.

### Highlights

- **Chat proxy.** YouTube, Klipy and other links load through a proxy, such as your VPN app's local port, while TeamSpeak stays connected directly.
- **HTTP or SOCKS5, with a login.** Find my VPN app fills in the port, Test says whether it works, and the proxy looks up the websites' names.
- **One click on and off.** The Plugins menu, a hotkey, /tsmedia proxy or Settings turn it on or off at once, without retyping anything.
- **Never a silent direct connection.** When the proxy fails, previews pause and one chat line offers Turn off chat proxy; nothing goes around it.
- **More VPN setups work.** Fake-IP TUN modes no longer break previews, and a site your network's DNS filters says so and offers the chat proxy.
- **Made by the sender, as before.** Previews you make through the proxy reach your channel from your TeamSpeak server, so friends need no VPN.

### What's new

- **Chat proxy** (*Plugins → TS Media chat → Turn on chat proxy*, or *Settings → Privacy & updates → Chat proxy settings…*): link previews (YouTube, Klipy and other GIF and media sites, cards for sites and pages, Nextcloud shares), the YouTube player and the update check and download go through an HTTP or SOCKS5 proxy with an optional user name and password. TeamSpeak's own connections never use it: the server connection, voice, chat, plugin commands and file transfers stay direct, so you can keep your VPN app's TUN or VPN mode off and stay connected to TeamSpeak servers in your country.
- **Easy to set up.** **Find my VPN app** looks for v2rayN, Throne, Nekoray, Clash Verge Rev and Hiddify at their usual ports on this PC (nothing leaves the PC), **Paste from clipboard** reads a link such as `socks5://127.0.0.1:10808`, and **Test** sends one request to `www.youtube.com` through the proxy and says what is wrong (*Can't reach the proxy at …*, *The proxy refused the login…*, *The proxy can't reach the website…*).
- **Names are looked up by the proxy**, never by your PC (SOCKS5 and HTTP `CONNECT` both get the name), so a filtered DNS doesn't matter and no DNS query for those sites leaves your PC.
- **On and off at once:** *Turn on chat proxy* / *Turn off chat proxy* in the Plugins menu, a TeamSpeak hotkey (*Turn the chat proxy on or off*), `/tsmedia proxy on` / `off` (`/tsmedia proxy` says what is in use) and a button in *Settings → Privacy & updates*. The proxy is kept while it is off, each switch says in the chat what it did, and previews that are loading start again on the new route. While it is off, TS Media uses your Windows proxy settings as before, or no proxy if you choose.
- **Fails closed.** When the chat proxy doesn't work, automatic previews show *Paused · the proxy isn't working* instead of error cards, and one line in the chat says why, with **Turn off chat proxy**. TS Media tries again by itself every 30 seconds (60 for a proxy on another computer), and the paused previews load once it works. After you turn it off, previews that waited for it load only when you click them.
- **The login** is optional: the password is stored with Windows DPAPI for your Windows account and only for the proxy it was saved with, and a proxy on another computer gets it only when it asks. It never appears in the log or the diagnostic info, whose new *Chat proxy* section names only the mode, where the proxy is and whether it works.

### Changed

- With *Use Windows proxy settings*, a Windows setting or setup script that names only a SOCKS proxy no longer sends previews directly: they wait for a click, and the Chat proxy window offers **Use it as the chat proxy**. For a SOCKS proxy in the Windows settings, the YouTube player doesn't play and the update check says it can't use that proxy.
- A link whose site your network's DNS filters (an answer in 10.10.34.0/24) says *Your network blocks this site* and offers **Use chat proxy** or **Set up proxy…**.
- *Play YouTube videos in the chat* is now *Play YouTube videos in a TS Media window*, and the update consent window names the YouTube player too.
- The GIFs bar says *Remove* instead of *Remove all* when there is only one GIF.

### Fixed

- Link previews with a fake-IP TUN VPN (Clash Verge Rev and other mihomo or sing-box TUN modes) no longer fail.
- A proxy that fails or is slow no longer pauses a website's previews for 10 minutes.
- A chat at its end stays there when previews are inserted or change size; after **Options → Addons → Reload** at 100% scaling it now stays at its end.

### Good to know

- **With a VPN app:** turn its TUN or VPN mode off, then press **Find my VPN app** (or enter `127.0.0.1` and the app's local port: v2rayN 10808, Throne and Nekoray 2080, Clash Verge Rev 7897, Hiddify 12334), **Test** and **OK**: [step by step](https://github.com/Metihttp/Teamspeak_Media_chat/blob/v2.6.0/docs/FAQ.md#how-do-i-use-ts-media-with-my-vpn-app).
- With the chat proxy on, Iranian sites go out through your VPN app's server abroad too; use the app's *bypass Iran* routing for them. TS Media has no bypass list of its own, on purpose.
- The proxy sees which websites TS Media contacts, but not the pages, which stay encrypted. Use a proxy you trust, normally your own VPN app on 127.0.0.1: [privacy & security](https://github.com/Metihttp/Teamspeak_Media_chat/blob/v2.6.0/docs/PRIVACY-SECURITY.md#chat-proxy).

### Install or update

**On 2.2.1 or later (2.2.1, 2.3.x, 2.4.0 or 2.5.0):** it updates itself. Wait for the update offer, or use **Check for updates…** in the plugin menu. Your settings, cache and GIFs are kept. On 2.2.0 it updates to 2.2.1 first, then to 2.6.0.

**On 2.1 or older, or new here?** Close TeamSpeak, double-click the file above, then type `/tsmedia` in the chat.

### Known issues

- The 32-bit DLL is untested on a real 32-bit client, and it has no YouTube player (Play opens your browser).
- After **Options → Addons → Reload** with Windows scaling above 100%, the chat may stop above its end, and a chat you scrolled up may move by a message or two.
- With *Use Windows proxy settings*, a Windows setup script that answers with a SOCKS proxy can still send the update check directly, and the YouTube player through SOCKS with names looked up on your PC; the chat proxy avoids this.
- TeamSpeak may crash or hang when it quits, with or without the plugin: [workaround](https://github.com/Metihttp/Teamspeak_Media_chat/blob/main/docs/FAQ.md#teamspeak-crashes-or-hangs-when-i-quit).
