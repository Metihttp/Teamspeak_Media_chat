**TS Media chat 2.3.0**: polls, live music, reactions on every message and a fresh, Telegram-like chat.

### Highlights

- **React to any message.** Text messages, links, replies and polls get emoji reactions too, not only media.
- **A paperclip in the chat input.** One click for Photo or video, Document, Poll and Live music.
- **Anonymous polls.** Ask your channel, vote on the card, see the results; nobody sees who voted for what.
- **Live music.** Play a song to your channel through your own voice and keep talking over it.
- **A fresh look.** Telegram-like message bubbles, centred system messages and a new send window.

![TS Media chat 2.3.0: a poll in the chat after voting, with percentages, counts, your choice ticked and reactions; the paperclip menu above the chat input with Photo or video, Document, Poll and Live music; and the music bar above the chat input while a song plays](https://github.com/Metihttp/Teamspeak_Media_chat/raw/v2.3.0/docs/images/polls-music-light.png)

### Fixed

- Your own avatar showed your initials: now TeamSpeak's own pictures are used, yours included.
- The voice recording strip could stay over another server tab's chat: now it stays on its own tab.
- **Add files…** in the send window did nothing: now it opens the file chooser.

### Good to know

- Live music sounds as good as the channel's codec allows: **Opus Music** is best, voice codecs make it sound thin.
- With push-to-talk the channel hears only the music while it plays. Use voice activation to talk over it.
- Live music pauses while you're on another server tab and continues when you come back.
- Polls are anonymous ballots, with honest limits: [how polls work](https://github.com/Metihttp/Teamspeak_Media_chat/blob/v2.3.0/docs/POLLS.md).
- Polls and reactions on messages need TS Media 2.3; everyone else reads a poll as text.

### Install or update

**On 2.2.x:** it updates itself. Wait for the update offer, or use **Check for updates…** in the plugin menu. Your settings and cache are kept.

**On 2.1 or older, or new here?** Close TeamSpeak, double-click the file above, then type `/tsmedia` in the chat.

### Known issues

- The 32-bit DLL is untested on a real 32-bit client.
- Messages without TeamSpeak's timestamps can't get reactions (timestamps are on by default).
- TeamSpeak may crash or hang when it quits, with or without the plugin: [workaround](https://github.com/Metihttp/Teamspeak_Media_chat/blob/main/docs/FAQ.md#teamspeak-crashes-or-hangs-when-i-quit).
